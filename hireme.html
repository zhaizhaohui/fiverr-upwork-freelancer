FAQ

How long does a page take?
Most single pages are delivered within 48 hours. Larger projects are split into milestones so you see progress early.
Do you support dark mode?
Yes — every page I build ships with both light and dark themes. A theme toggle is included, and the user's choice is remembered.
What if I need changes after delivery?
Minor revisions are free for 7 days after delivery. Bigger changes are quoted separately — always confirmed before I start.
Which platform should I use?
Fiverr for fixed-scope, quick projects. Upwork for ongoing work or hourly contracts. Freelancer for larger or less-defined projects where you want competing bids.
Which tech stacks do you work with?
Plain HTML/CSS, Bootstrap 5, Tailwind CSS, Layui, and React + TypeScript. If you need a specific stack, just ask.
